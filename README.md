# ***Mobile Suit Gundam 0080: War in the Pocket***
---
### Overview
> Mobile Suit Gundam 0080: War in the Pocket (or simply known as War in the Pocket) follows Alfred Izuruha, an eleven-year-old boy in Side 6, a neutral space colony whose fascination with mobile suits and views war with rose-tinted glasses leads him into an uncanny friendship with Zeon rookie pilot Bernard "Bernie" Wiseman and special forces unit "Cyclops Team" during the final days of the One Year War.
### Production & Release
> Produced by animation studio Sunrise, this six-episode series is directed by Fumihiko Takayama and written by Hiroyuki Yamaga with character designs by Haruhiko Mikimoto. It was released as an **original video animation** (OVA) in 1989 as the first OVA series in the *Gundam* franchise.
### Reception
> This short and bittersweet series is praised for its emotional depth, anti-war message, and departure from typical franchise tropes, making it a highly recommended entry point into Gundam, ~~especially during Christmastime.~~
